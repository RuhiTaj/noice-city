# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful project where people can discuss
voice technology without harassment, intimidation, or discrimination. That
commitment applies regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

Voice technology carries unusual consent and impersonation risks. Participation
in this community requires respecting the agency, privacy, and safety of every
person whose voice could be recorded, modeled, or discussed.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Respect boundaries and requests to stop an interaction.
- Obtain explicit permission before using another person's voice or personal
  material in a demonstration.
- Use synthetic or fully consented examples, and remove private data from logs,
  screenshots, issues, and test fixtures.
- Disclose meaningful limitations and do not present generated speech as a real
  person's statement.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information or voice data without clear
  permission.
- Using the project to impersonate, defraud, intimidate, or evade consent.
- Pressuring anyone to provide recordings or biometric-like voice artifacts.
- Deliberately disrupting project spaces or retaliating against a good-faith
  report.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other spaces where someone is representing the project or its community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not place identifying details from a conduct report in a public issue. Contact
the repository owner through a private GitHub channel. If no suitable private
channel is available, open a minimal issue asking the maintainer to establish
private contact, without naming people or describing the incident publicly.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue a warning,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban someone whose conduct is
incompatible with this policy. Reports will be handled as privately as
practicable, with attention to the safety of the person reporting.
