prominently than your own name, or present an unofficial distribution as an
official Noice City release.

release, replace the Noice City name, logo, app icon, and other Noice City
branding with your own. A fork may state in plain text that it is “forked from
Noice City” when that statement is accurate, but it must not suggest that the
fork is maintained, sponsored, or endorsed by the Noice City project.

Written permission is required for any other use, including use in a product or
service name, package name, domain, social account, advertising, merchandise,

request permission or clarify a proposed use.
